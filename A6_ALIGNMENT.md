## Why the full marketing plan is not pasted into public pages
The six-week calendar, channel frequencies, ownership, governance and measurement framework direct how the team promotes the site. They are not customer-facing product capabilities. The website provides the matching content and conversion surfaces without presenting an unexecuted campaign as evidence.
