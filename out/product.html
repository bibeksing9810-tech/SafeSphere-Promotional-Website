What SafeSphere is

Information becomes useful when it can be practised.

The proposed product combines interactive environments, scenario logic and structured results into one training loop.

CAPABILITY

Interactive 3D Training

A browser-based 3D environment turns safety content into something the learner can explore and act within.

CAPABILITY

Hazard Perception

Hazards are discovered as part of the workplace rather than isolated inside a quiz.

CAPABILITY

Decision-Making

Learners choose what should happen next, creating a clearer link between knowledge and action.

CAPABILITY

Immediate Feedback

The proposed experience explains the safety meaning of an interaction directly after the decision.

CAPABILITY

Structured Results

Outcomes can be represented through completion, performance, decisions and attempt history.

CAPABILITY

Trainer Visibility

Training personnel can be presented with a structured view of learner outcomes.
