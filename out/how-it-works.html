How it works

Experience safety training as a process, not a page.

The proposed SafeSphere learning loop connects assignment, interaction, decision-making and measurement.

The full journey

Assign → Enter → Interact → Decide → Measure → Review → Progress

01

Assign

A trainer or manager selects the appropriate training experience for the learner.

02

Enter

The learner opens the proposed desktop-browser experience and receives a scenario briefing.

03

Interact

The learner moves through the simulated environment and investigates the workplace.

04

Decide

The learner identifies hazards or unsafe conditions and chooses an appropriate response.

05

Measure

The training outcome is recorded and presented as structured performance information.

06

Review

The result creates a basis for learner reflection and trainer review.

07

Progress

Achievement, mastery and completion make development visible.
