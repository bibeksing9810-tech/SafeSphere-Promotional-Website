Designed product journey

From assignment to improvement.

  1. Log in to a role-based account
  2. Review the dashboard
  3. Open assigned training
  4. Read the scenario briefing
  5. Enter frozen storage
  6. Explore the environment
  7. Notice a hazard
  8. Inspect or interact
  9. Choose a safety response
  10. Read the explanation
  11. View the server-scored result
  12. Review progress and improve

This sequence describes the proposed training application. This promotional site does not authenticate learners, assign training or store assessed results.
